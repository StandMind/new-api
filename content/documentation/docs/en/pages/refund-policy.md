# Refund Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026. This policy describes how we review refund requests for Aivrae prepaid credits.

## General policy

Aivrae sells prepaid credits for API usage. Refunds are reviewed case by case. Credits already consumed by API requests are generally non-refundable because service costs may already have been incurred.

## Eligible refund requests

Duplicate payments, failed credit delivery, billing mistakes, or unused credit requests submitted shortly after purchase may be eligible for review.

## Non-refundable cases

Consumed API credits, policy violations, suspended accounts, unsupported third-party clients, invalid prompts, or model service limitations are generally non-refundable.

## How to request a refund

Email support@aivrae.com with your account email, transaction reference, requested amount, and reason. We aim to review refund requests within 3 business days.
