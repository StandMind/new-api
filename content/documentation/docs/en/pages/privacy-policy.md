# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026. This policy explains how Aivrae collects, uses, and protects information when you use the website, console, and API gateway.

## Information we collect

We collect account details, authentication data, billing and credit records, API keys, request metadata, usage logs, device information, and support communications.

## How we use information

We use information to provide the service, authenticate users, process credits, meter API usage, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

## API content and third-party model providers

API requests may be routed to third-party model providers selected by Aivrae. Those providers may process request content according to their own terms and privacy practices.

## Data retention

We retain account and usage records as long as necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, enforce policies, and comply with legal or accounting requirements.

## Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of account information, subject to security, fraud prevention, legal, and billing record requirements.
