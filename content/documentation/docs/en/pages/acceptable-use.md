# Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026. This policy applies to all use of Aivrae, including API requests, accounts, credits, and integrations.

## Principle

Aivrae is designed for lawful, authorized AI API usage. Users are responsible for ensuring prompts, outputs, integrations, and business uses comply with applicable laws and third-party model service policies.

## Prohibited activity

Illegal activity, fraud, scams, phishing, spam, malware, credential theft, unauthorized access, pornography, sexually explicit or NSFW content, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate content, hate, threats, violence, self-harm instructions, or attempts to evade provider restrictions are prohibited.

## Enforcement

We may rate limit, suspend, or terminate accounts and API keys that violate this policy. We may preserve records and cooperate with payment providers, third-party model providers, or lawful requests when necessary.

## Reporting abuse

Report suspected abuse to support@aivrae.com. Include relevant account, request, or transaction details if available.
